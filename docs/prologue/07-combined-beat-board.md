# PANTHEON PROLOGUE — COMBINED BEAT BOARD

Three parts, one arc, **~3:20** before the movie proper begins.

```
SPEED  ->  UNDERSTANDING  ->  SCALE  ->  HISTORY  ->  THE MOVIE
```

| | Part | Runs | Ends on | Hands over by |

Each seam is **built by the preceding part**, not bridged by a transition
effect. Part 1 ends by naming the arena. Part 2 ends by repeating a round until
repetition is the point. Part 3 opens on that same grid, already multiplying.
**No fade-to-black between parts** (P1-H).

---

## WHAT THE VIEWER UNDERSTANDS, CUMULATIVELY

**After Part 1** — Quake is fast, and the speed is *earned* rather than given.
Movement is a weapon. Weapons do different jobs. Something is going to happen in
an arena.

**After Part 2** — a round is two teams, one life each, everything in hand from
the start, and it is over in about 25 seconds. Then it happens again. They can
now *read* the footage they were shown in Part 1.

**After Part 3** — someone recorded eleven years of this. A machine made it
searchable. A human decided what each moment was for. The film they are about
to watch is the result of those decisions — not a compilation.

## THE THREE SHOTS THAT CARRY THE FILM

If everything else were cut, these three would still make the argument:

1. **0:07.5 — the 1.2 s silence.** Proves *fast does not mean chaotic*
   structurally rather than by assertion. Without it Part 1 is noise.
2. **0:59.5 — the pip that goes dark and stays dark.** Teaches one-life-per-round
   without a card. The card that follows only confirms it.
3. **2:29 — the annotation becoming the effect.** The only shot that shows human
   judgement actually driving the picture. It is the thesis, visible.

## WHAT IS DELIBERATELY WITHHELD

The prologue foreshadows `TACTICAL_ROUND_STORY`, projectile camera,
`TELEPORT_TRANSITION`, material transformation and rhythmic weapon sequences —
each for at most 1.5 s, once.

It **does not spend**: `GRENADE_LAUNCH_AND_IMPACT_SEQUENCE`, enemy POV as a
developed device, x-ray, any 2D information graphic beyond the primitives above,
or any world transformation past the Part 2 map build.

**The montage's job is to make the viewer believe there is more — not to show
them what it is.**
