# QUAKE LEGACY - Media Post Preparation

**Sequence:** best legacy clips -> project PDF -> suite completion -> newly
generated cinematic work.

The footage leads. The PDF explains what viewers have already seen. New
generation begins only after the suite passes its review gates.

## 1. Wave one - best clips

Select **three to five** finished videos from the existing clip batch. Do not
begin with a compilation of everything. The first posts should establish three
qualities:

1. the gameplay is worth preserving;
2. the archive has visual variety;
3. the existing footage is real proof, not a future-product mockup.

### Selection criteria

- strongest opening 10 seconds;
- readable action without explanation;
- distinct pacing or weapon identity across the set;
- clean audio and full decode already verified;
- no accidental personal information in thumbnails or descriptions;
- no claim that these legacy videos demonstrate unfinished Pandora effects.

### Recommended order

1. **Immediate-impact video** - fastest proof that the archive is worth seeing.
2. **Movement or multi-angle video** - expands the visual vocabulary.
3. **Longer-flow video** - demonstrates pacing and music rather than one frag.
4. Optional: a contrasting atmospheric or unusual selection.
5. Optional: the strongest personal favorite, with a short reason.

### Short caption

> I kept years of Quake Live recordings and recently finished preserving the
> best of the old clip archive. I am posting a few of those films first. The
> larger project is a suite that reads the original demos, finds the moments,
> directs the replay and eventually builds new cinematic sequences from them.

> This is the footage first. The system behind it comes next.

### Per-video caption

> One selection from the QUAKE LEGACY archive. This comes from the completed
> legacy clip batch; it is not a generated Pandora scene. I am sharing the best
> finished footage first, then the project overview, then the new work once the
> creative suite is ready.

## 2. Wave two - share the PDF

Share `Quake Legacy - Media Edition.pdf` only after at least two strong videos
are public. The PDF is context, not the opening hook.

### PDF post caption

> The clips are the proof. This is the project behind them.

> QUAKE LEGACY starts with the original demo files rather than finished video.
> It turns recorded game state into searchable moments, music-aware timing,
> directed camera work, effects and Quake-native transitions. The parser and
> core runtime are real; the full creative suite is still being completed and
> reviewed before new generation begins.

> PDF: [attach]
> Video: https://www.youtube.com/@Stony_
> Project: https://github.com/Stoneface30/quake-legacy

### PDF hosting rule

Upload the PDF as a normal static document. Do **not** rely on embedded video:
rich-media PDF playback is inconsistent across phones, browsers, Discord and
social platforms. The PDF contains clickable video/project links; the moving
camera and effects are demonstrated in video.

## 3. Wave three - suite completion

Do not announce public generation merely because individual primitives work.
Before this wave:

- editorial canaries pass user review;
- FPV-first and replay grammar is stable;
- event truth and provenance are visible in the review artifacts;
- music alignment is perceptually reviewed, not only numerically aligned;
- camera/effect changes preserve duration and gameplay truth;
- final assembly has a repeatable review -> approve -> render path;
- the public repository passes privacy and large-file checks.

### Status language before completion

Use:

> The parser, semantic scene model, music analysis and camera foundations are
> working. The complete creative suite is still in editorial validation.

Avoid:

- "one click makes a fragmovie";
- fixed per-clip generation-time promises;
- `pip install` or public release claims before packaging exists;
- saying every effect in the atlas is implemented;
- describing a canary or schema as a finished production renderer.

## 4. Wave four - newly generated work

The first generated post should be a small before/after proof:

1. clean FPV event;
2. reviewed directed version;
3. one diagnostic frame explaining the event/music/camera decision;
4. a concise description of what was generated and what remained original.

Only after that proof should the project publish longer sequences or the more
ambitious effect families: projectile bridges, teleporter passes, geometry
matches, material transitions, 1vX treatments and motif montages.

## 5. Thumbnail and asset checklist

- 16:9 thumbnail, readable at phone size;
- one subject and one short phrase, not a dashboard screenshot;
- no opponent/player names in public repo imagery;
- no scoreboard, obituary feed or chat unless deliberately reviewed for the
  social post and kept outside the public repository;
- consistent PANTHEON black / silver / yellow treatment;
- retain a clean master, upload copy and caption text for every post;
- record URL, date, platform and final caption in a release log.

## 7. Final publication gate

Immediately before each post:

- watch the exact uploaded file from beginning to end;
- verify title, thumbnail, description and links on mobile;
- confirm every number against the current artifact index;
- confirm the post distinguishes legacy batch footage from newly generated
  material;
- run the public-repo privacy sweep;
- publish to one primary community first and respond before cross-posting.
